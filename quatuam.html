Quantum Knowledge

Qubit: The basic unit of quantum information.

Superposition: A quantum state can be a combination of |0⟩ and |1⟩.

Hadamard (H): Creates or changes superposition.

Pauli-X (X): Swaps |0⟩ and |1⟩.

Pauli-Z (Z): Changes the phase of the |1⟩ amplitude.

Measurement: Produces 0 or 1 according to their respective probabilities and collapses the simulated state.
